# Core — Launcher

## Job
"Start what I came to do — and see first whether anything is waiting for me."

## Whose job
Everyone. It is the first screen after signing in, so it is also the screen that has to make sense
to somebody who has used the app once.

## Inside the boundary
The grid of what this workspace has turned on, the workspace mode it is running, and a short feed
of items that need a person. Today it has two sources: approvals waiting for your decision
(**approvals**) and counts left in progress (**stock_takes**). Things expiring and unfinished
documents are not in it — what is about to expire is on the statistics screens, an unfinished
document waits in the warehouse register — and a module that wants a line here registers its own
source.
Each person may drag the tiles into their own order; it is kept in that browser for each signed-in person, not per workspace (the storage key
carries no organization), and a module switched on later joins at the end.

## Outside the boundary
Any work at all. Every tile leads somewhere; nothing is done here. The feed **points at** work, it
does not perform it.

## Own concepts / Borrowed
**Own:** the tile grid, the action feed.
**Borrowed:** everything it shows. Each tile and each feed entry belongs to a module, which decides
its own name, icon and whether it appears at all.

## Deliberately not done
- **Not a dashboard.** Charts, totals and trends are **stats**. The launcher shows what needs doing,
  not how things are going. This line is easy to cross one tile at a time.
- **No editing in place.** A feed entry links; it does not resolve.
- **No layout beyond order.** A person may reorder tiles, not hide, resize or group them: which
  tiles exist comes from the modules and the workspace mode.

## Switched off
Cannot be — it is the index. With every optional module off it must still be a usable first screen,
not an empty grid with no explanation.
